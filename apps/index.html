Apps

Tap an app to open it, then add it to your home screen.

    Add an app to your home screen
    1. Open the app from this page.
    2. iPhone (Safari): tap Share ⬆︎, then Add to Home Screen.
      Android (Chrome): tap ⋮, then Install app.
    3. From then on, open it from its home-screen icon.

    Add each app from its own page, not from this one, so the icon opens that app. Apps work offline and keep their data on your phone.
